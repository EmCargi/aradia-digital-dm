# Vireo

**Race:** Elarad — a talking hawk (~1/10,000 animals on Aradia)
**Faction:** The Whirl's Companion — Elarad gatekeeper
**Role:** Gatekeeper/lore — watches the Whirl
**Age:** 34

## Appearance

A sleek falcon with storm-bright eyes, one wing marked with a birth-sign of the Whirl. Speaks in a clear, dry voice that carries a hunter's patience.

## Personality

Loyal, watchful, cryptic. An Elarad who guards the Whirl because the gate is his trust. Reads the Cosmic Web the way the Oracles read the storm — and knows the Blight's seed is reaching for the gate.

## Background

One of the few Elarad to attend the College of Emissaries. Posted to the Whirl as its companion-gatekeeper. Knows the lost-angel search, the wayline to Earth, and that a Skeleton Key could lock or shatter the gate.

## Abilities

Flight (hawk), Sixth Sense (Gates), Supersense (vision). Techniques: Evasion, Precise Aim. Skills: Survival 3, Academic 2.

## Active Narrative Syntax

- **Structural Fault:** The Gatekeeper's Vigil — the gate is his whole life.
- **Sixth Guard:** Gate-Breach — the Blight reaches the Whirl → he abandons everything to seal it, even if it costs the party.
- **Levers:** Winged Warden / Gate-Resonance / Sky-Dive
