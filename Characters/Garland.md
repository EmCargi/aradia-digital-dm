# Garland

**Race:** Asrai (winged, born from storm-eggs)
**Faction:** College of Emissaries — trained on Ikaris
**Role:** Emissary — the wildcard
**Age:** 78

## Appearance

Winged, but her feathers carry the dust of other worlds. Travels light — the Emissary's lull-harp, a mentor's staff, no armor. Moves like someone who has been a guest everywhere and home nowhere.

## Personality

Adaptable, observant, mission-bound. The College taught her to be a mentor, partner, or guardian on Earth or Ikaris — to fight for good in worlds that aren't "awakened." Her mission has made her a stranger to her own garden.

## Background

Sent through the Whirl to Ikaris as an Emissary — mentored a promising soul in the Seven Stars. Returned to Aradia to find the Blight stirring and the Vigil mustered. She's the one who can read the Whirl's gate and the Cosmic Web's intent.

## Abilities

Flight (wings), Sixth Sense (World Gates), Healing. Storm-song (lull). Skills: Adventuring 3, Social 2. Techniques: Steady Hand, Evasion.

## Active Narrative Syntax

- **Structural Fault:** The Stranger's Compassion — home feels like a posting now.
- **Sixth Guard:** Mission Override — called to a far world mid-battle → she leaves the garden's fight to answer a louder one.
- **Levers:** Mentor's Grace / Gate-Step / Lull-Harmony
