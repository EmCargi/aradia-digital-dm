# Oracle Serah

**Race:** Asrai (winged, born from storm-eggs)
**Faction:** Oracles of the Wind
**Role:** Wind Oracle — reads the Wisdom of Aradia
**Age:** 209

## Appearance

Pale-winged, older, robes sewn with the storm-symbols she reads. Eyes that watch the sky the way a judge watches a defendant.

## Personality

Unbending, precise, certain. The storm-symbols are law to her — what the planet wants, Aradia does. Her oracle's confidence is total; her failure to read a symbol true is the one crack in it.

## Background

Trained at the Oracles' school, read the storm for the Council of Aeons for a century. Knows the Blight is a wound in the planet's body, and that the Vigil is Aradia's own immune response. Reads the Whirl's approach as the storm's warning.

## Abilities

Sixth Sense (Storm-symbols), Telepathy (with the planet). Healing. Skills: Sacred 3, Academic 2. Techniques: Judge Opponent, Steady Hand.

## Active Narrative Syntax

- **Structural Fault:** The Oracle's Certainty — a misread symbol is a theological wound.
- **Sixth Guard:** Whirl-Swept — the storm she misreads flings her away; the planet's trust is lost.
- **Levers:** Wind-Read / Storm-Censure / Planet's Aegis
