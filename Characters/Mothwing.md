# Mothwing

**Race:** Fairy (10–15cm, insect-winged) — **tainted by the Bane**
**Faction:** Tainted — fled into the Wormwood Blight
**Role:** Moral thrall — redeem-or-destroy
**Age:** 33 (fairy years)

## Appearance

Tiny, wings gone moth-grey, veins darkening at the tips. Carries the Blight's cancer-seed inside her like a splinter of the demonic Bane. Beautiful, wrong, afraid.

## Personality

Frayed, guilty, still-good underneath. The Bane taint whispers to her, but Mothwing is fighting it. She fled into the Blight to keep the seed from reaching the Whirl — but she's running out of will.

## Background

A fairy of Aradia who brushed against the Wormwood Blight's edge and came away carrying the cancer-seed. Her Bane Hunter kin couldn't bring themselves to destroy her, so she ran. The Vigil must decide: redeem her (the Lull-Harp's mercy) or destroy her (the War-Harp's duty).

## Abilities

Flight (insect-wings), Dark-Vision (taint), Sixth Sense (Bane). Powers: Enervation (taint-slip), Healing (against the taint). Defects: Tainted (Bane), Fragile 1.

## Active Narrative Syntax

- **Structural Fault:** The Splinter — the Bane taint is a seed that wants to bloom.
- **Sixth Guard:** Bloom — the taint overwhelms her → she becomes the Blight's herald, and the Vigil must destroy her.
- **Levers:** Taint-Hold / Desperate Hum / Moth-Grace
