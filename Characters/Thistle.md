# Thistle

**Race:** Fairy (10–15cm, insect-winged)
**Faction:** Bane Hunters — fairy side
**Role:** Broker — knows the Blight's dark fairies
**Age:** 40 (fairy years)

## Appearance

Tiny, iridescent-winged, perched on a leaf-stalk like a gossamer grenade. Carries a thimble-cup and a voice bigger than her body.

## Personality

Wry, sharp, mischievous-but-good. A fairy who knows every dark fairy in the Blight by name — and every way to bribe, trick, or out-fly them. Loathes the Bane that tainted her kin.

## Background

Rescued from Arcadia as an egg, raised on Aradia after the Bane ate her home world. Joined the fairy Bane Hunters who track the tainted. When Mothwing slipped into the Blight, Thistle knew the way in — and the way out.

## Abilities

Flight (insect-wings), Concealment (fairy smallness), Sixth Sense (taint). Techniques: Concealment, Portable Armoury. Skills: Street 3, Social 2, Survival 2.

## Active Narrative Syntax

- **Structural Fault:** The Survivor's Grudge — the Bane that ate Arcadia is personal.
- **Sixth Guard:** Kin-Betrayal — a fairy she trusted turns tainted → she flees the garden rather than fight kin.
- **Levers:** Gossamer Slip / Taint-Whisper / Thorn-Bargain
