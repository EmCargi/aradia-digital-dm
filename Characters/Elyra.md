# Elyra

**Race:** Asrai (winged, born from storm-eggs)
**Faction:** The Sabaoth — warrior angels
**Role:** Hunter — prime-faithful of the Vigil
**Age:** 112

## Appearance

Winged, storm-eyed, a Sabaoth war-harp slung across her back. Feathers catch the light of the Tiara of Aradia. Moves like the wind she was born from.

## Personality

Dedicated, fierce, loyal to the Vigil. The Sabaoth's warrior training is her whole art — hunting demons is what she was hatched to do. Her oath is her soul; the Blight is her wound to tend.

## Background

Born from a storm-egg, raised in a Sabaoth encampment, trained to fight demons. She hunted the Bane's remnants in the Blight's edge for decades. When a tainted fairy (Mothwing) slipped past the Ward, Elyra swore the Vigil and took the hunt.

## Abilities

Flight (wings), Healing, Storm-song. Techniques: Precise Aim, Lightning Reflexes. Skills: Adventuring 2, Survival 2.
