# The Whirl — Aradia's Gate to Earth

**Type:** Natural dimensional gate — a whirlpool in the ocean
**Wayline:** Aradia ↔ Earth (one of Aradia's 7 waylines)
**Role:** Carries Asrai eggs to Earth; Emissaries' search lane for "lost angels"

The Whirl is Aradia's World Gate — a natural feature, not a machine. Every so often an Asrai egg falls in and washes ashore on Earth. Emissaries search for these lost angels for years or decades. The demo's lore node — the party discovers the Blight's seed is trying to reach the Whirl.

**Mechanics:** A `lore` node with a `Sixth Sense (Gates)` reader (Garland's Emissary training) — the gate is real, and it's the Blight's target.
