# The Vale of Thorns & Sabaoth Encampment

**Type:** The Council of Aeons' green — a Sabaoth hunting encampment on the Vale's edge
**Hook:** The 6-node starter's hub (`node_01_vale`)

The Vale of Thorns is the political heart of Aradia — the Chamber of Aeons, the Festival of the Arch, and the Sabaoth's muster. The demo opens in a Sabaoth encampment watching the Wormwood Blight's horizon.
