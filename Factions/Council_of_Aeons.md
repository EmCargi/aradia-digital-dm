# The Council of Aeons

**Leader:** 101 elected members, Chamber of Aeons, Vale of Thorns
**Election:** Festival of the Arch, first day of spring each year
**Doctrine:** The ruling body of the Asrai — the Wisdom of Aradia made law

The Council of Aeons meets in the Chamber of Aeons in the Vale of Thorns. Its 101 members are elected at the Festival of the Arch. It is the political hub of Aradia — the Sabaoth reports directly to it, and the Emissaries' missions are weighed against the Wisdom of Aradia.

**Cast hook:** **Elyra** (Sabaoth hunter) reports to the Council; the demo's hub is a Sabaoth encampment on the Vale's edge.
