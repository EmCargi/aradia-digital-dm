# The Oracles of the Wind

**Type:** Priests of Aradia
**Role:** Interpret the Wisdom of Aradia — the symbol-language of storms and clouds
**Trust:** Guide the pastoral Asrai, advise the Council, read the weather of the world's will

The Oracles read the planet's communication. The storm-symbols are a unique language; the Oracles are its speakers. In a world with no economy, the Oracles' interpretations are the closest thing to governance by consensus — what the planet wants, the Asrai do.

**Cast hook:** **Oracle Serah** (Wind Oracle) — the contrarian enforcer who reads the storm as law, and knows the Blight is a wound in the planet's body.
