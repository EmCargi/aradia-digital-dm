# The College of Emissaries

**Type:** Training school for specially selected Asrai
**Mission:** Sent to other worlds to further the cause of good
**Role:** Mentor, partner, or guardian to promising individuals on Earth or Ikaris
**Also:** Sends Elarad (talking animals) as familiars for "magical girls"

Part of the Wisdom of Aradia is the knowledge that many worlds are not "awakened" — their world-spirits sleep, trapped in nightmares by evil. The College trains Asrai to be Emissaries: sent to those worlds to help overcome the evil. An Emissary often acts as mentor to a particularly promising individual — the "magical girl" pipeline.

**Cast hook:** **Garland** (College of Emissaries, trained on Ikaris) — the wildcard whose mission has made her a stranger to home.
