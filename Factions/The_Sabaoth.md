# The Sabaoth

**Type:** The armed Host of the Asrai — "warrior angels"
**Founded:** ~600 years ago, after the Bane incursion
**Chain:** Reports directly to the Council of Aeons; instructs Emissaries in fighting
**Missions:** Difficult and dangerous — fighting demons

The Sabaoth is Aradia's fighting order, warrior angels with extensive military training. Sabaoth Asrai are also Emissaries, trusted with demon-fighting missions. The **Bane Hunters** — Asrai and fairies who hunt the tainted — are the Sabaoth's frontline response to the Blight.

**Cast hook:** **Elyra** (Sabaoth hunter, winged Asrai) — the demo's prime-faithful, sworn to the Vigil.
