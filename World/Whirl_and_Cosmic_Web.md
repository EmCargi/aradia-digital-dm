# The Whirl & The Cosmic Web

> **Source:** BESM 4e Chapter 14, pp. 315 (Aradia) + pp. 309–311 (Cosmic Web).

## The Whirl

The **Whirl** is a natural dimensional gate that leads to **Earth**. It is a whirlpool that sometimes appears in the ocean. Every so often an Asrai egg falls into the Whirl instead of open sea — the egg is carried through the gate and reappears on Earth, washing ashore. Once touched, the egg dissolves, leaving a baby who can pass for human (wings don't manifest until adolescence).

**One of the jobs of Emissaries visiting Earth is to search for these occasional "lost angels."**

## Aradia's Waylines

Aradia has **seven waylines**: one to Earth, two to Inner Worlds, and four to all the other Prime Worlds save Bazaroth. Emissaries often visit Earth and Ikaris in stealthy fashion. Elarad who attended the College of Emissaries are sometimes sent to Earth with items of power as familiars and mentors for "magical girls" fighting the minions of Bazaroth.

## Cast Hook

The demo's lore node is **the Whirl** — the gate to Earth, humming with the Cosmic Web. The party learns the Vigil isn't just about a tainted fairy; the Blight's seed is trying to reach the gate.
