# Aradia — The Living Heaven

> **Source:** BESM 4e Chapter 14, pp. 314–315 (Aradia).

## Overview

Aradia is the "Living Heaven" — an Earth-like world, two-thirds ocean, six continents, most covered in dense sylvan forests. Unspoiled by cities or mines. Clear air, a billion stars. A shattered moon of ice and rock (the **Tiara of Aradia**) forms a great arch across the sky; four tiny "shepherd moons" orbit as moving sparks of light.

## A Living Being

Aradia is not just a beautiful planet — **it is a living being.** The natives believe it is a manifestation of the Weaver and call it the **Womb of Life**. The planet communicates its desires — the **Wisdom of Aradia** — through the movement of clouds and storms upon its surface. These patterns form a unique language of symbols that its priests, the **Oracles of the Wind**, interpret.

## Mythical Ecology

The flora and fauna resemble Earth's, but Aradia is also home to many exalted mythical creatures: **unicorns, winged horses, gryphons, sea serpents**. Some are fierce enough to snack on an unwary traveler, but none are innately evil. This is the purest "good" world in the Multiverse.
