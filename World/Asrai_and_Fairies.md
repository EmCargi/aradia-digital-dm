# The Asrai & Fairies

> **Source:** BESM 4e Chapter 14, pp. 314–315 (Aradia).

## The Asrai

The dominant species of Aradia. They resemble humans except for **small feathered wings that can expand out to full size** as necessary. Highly magical, though some are more talented than others. They are born "from the dreams of Aradia":

- When two Asrai mate, the female does not become pregnant. Their passion triggers a **lightning storm in the clouds**, and eventually an egg forms in the sky.
- The egg falls into the ocean, washes ashore, and hatches.
- Away from Aradia, wings take a long time to form — usually not manifesting until adolescence (so a "lost angel" on Earth can pass for human).

Asrai civilization is largely **pastoral** — they farm, build, herd, hunt, but do not mine. Most live in small villages or isolated houses. There is **no economy as such**, just exchanges of goods for mutual benefit.

## Fairies

Fairies are **tiny 10–15 cm insect-winged pixies**, rescued from Arcadia, an Outer World corrupted by the Bane. There are now as many on Aradia as there are Asrai (~half a million), most living in the woods. They are typically good, though mischievous — but **a few were tainted by the Bane and turned evil**. The Asrai and the fairies' own **Bane Hunters** deal with these problems.

## Cast Hooks

- **Elyra** — Sabaoth hunter, winged Asrai
- **Thistle** — 10cm fairy broker who knows the Blight's dark fairies
- **Mothwing** — Bane-tainted fairy, the redeem-or-destroy moral thrall
