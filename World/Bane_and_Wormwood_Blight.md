# The Bane & Wormwood Blight

> **Source:** BESM 4e Chapter 14, pp. 314–315 (Aradia).

## The Bane

The **Bane** is a demonic power that transformed the fairy world **Arcadia** (an Outer World), sucking away its life. The fairies were rescued to Aradia. The Bane also struck Aradia itself — a demonic incursion that led to the founding of the **Sabaoth** (~600 years ago).

## Wormwood Blight

Wormwood Blight is **part of the price Aradia paid** to rescue the fairies. It is a **cosmic cancer** that "crossed over" — an entire forested mountain valley now infected and corrupted by the evil Bane. The area is small, and Aradia's energies have contained it — but not destroyed it.

**Evil forces exist within:** terrible illusions, **dark fairies, cannibal unicorns, wyverns**, and other creatures of evil. Wayward seeds of Wormwood Blight have occasionally sprung up elsewhere; the Asrai always destroy them.

## Cast Hook

The demo patrol is a **Vigil of the Blight** — tracking a tainted fairy (Mothwing) who has slipped into the Blight's edge before the cancer spreads. This is Aradia's dungeon: the one place in the Living Heaven that isn't living.
